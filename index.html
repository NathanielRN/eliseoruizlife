About me

Machine learning is only useful once it survives production.

I am a University of Waterloo Computer Engineering graduate who took the Artificial Intelligence option because I wanted to build systems that learn. Five years of industry work since then have made me an expert in everything that happens after a model is accurate — latency budgets, memory ceilings, offline evaluations, and the careful milestone plans that move research code into a mainline hundreds of stores depend on — and I have a track record of landing exactly that kind of launch.

That is the work I do now as an SDE 2 on Amazon Just Walk Out. I have been release lead on three algorithm launches — a streaming re-architecture, a Transformer-based beverage model, and a neural network that replaced a random forest classifier — each one coordinated across several engineers and researchers, and each one measured in latency, accuracy, and dollars saved.

Before Just Walk Out I worked on AWS X-Ray and OpenTelemetry, where most of my code was open source and reviewed in public. Six co-op terms before that took me through Newfront Insurance, Electronic Arts, Splunk, Yahoo!, and Rave — front end, game tooling, iOS, and backend in turn. I am happiest on the part of a problem where the measurement and the engineering meet.
